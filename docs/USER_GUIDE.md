# Farmer guide

Register with your name, Eswatini mobile number, region, nearest weather town, and farm activity. Crop farmers add a crop and soil details; other activities do not require a crop. The dashboard shows a weather-based first check and a daily operations checklist. Crop tasks include water planning, weeding, soil and fertilizer decisions, pest and disease scouting, and work for the saved growth stage. Livestock, poultry, fisheries, and beekeeping profiles get activity-specific tasks. Crop fit and yield estimates appear for crop farmers. Use soil-test results and local extension recommendations for fertilizer rates and crop treatments; the dashboard does not prescribe chemical doses. Eswatini's [Ministry of Agriculture resources](https://agrinfosystems.gov.sz/Quality.php) include production guidelines, soil sampling, and crop storage materials. Farmers who opt in receive a short farm-operations summary by SMS.
